How direction and matching work
Each GPS fix is projected onto the nearest segment of the ordered Columbia Pike signal corridor. Direction uses recent change in matched corridor distance—not a single noisy point. A new direction needs repeated movement evidence, while reversing an established direction requires a larger opposite displacement and several consecutive confirmations. Direction is held while stopped instead of rapidly flipping.

All observations remain in this browser until you download the CSV or reload the page. No location data is transmitted by this file.
